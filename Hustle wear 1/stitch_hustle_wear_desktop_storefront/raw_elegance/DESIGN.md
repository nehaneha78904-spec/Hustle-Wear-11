## Brand & Style

This design system defines a luxury contemporary streetwear aesthetic rooted in modern Indian subculture and architectural minimalism. It bridges raw urban momentum with high-end editorial curation. The target audience encompasses culturally discerning youth, creative entrepreneurs, and fashion purists who value structural silhouettes, artisanal textiles, and uncompromised digital execution.

The visual style merges **Minimalist Editorial** with subtle **Brutalist Architecture**:
- Generous, intentional off-white whitespace that lets dramatic garment photography command the viewport.
- Razor-sharp, disciplined gridlines and hairline borders reminiscent of technical apparel patterns and gallery lookbooks.
- Expressive, structured display typography juxtaposed against a clinical, utilitarian grotesque sans-serif for functional UI.
- Tactile, tangible interactions: crisp borders, zero heavy blurs, and immediate, physical hover states that evoke bespoke packaging and gallery installations.

## Colors

The palette establishes a high-fashion, organic-architectural tone. Built over a warm, tactile foundation, high-contrast typography and unexpected editorial accents create an unmistakable presence:

- **Canvas Foundation (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm Ivory / Rice Paper. Replaces sterile digital white with a tactile, editorial paper tone that enriches garment textures.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white reserved strictly for product cards, floating panels, and drawers to create clean, luminous separation against the ivory canvas.
- **Primary Accent (`#8FA842` - Kinetic Olive / Acid Lime):** An energetic, sharp streetwear accent used intentionally for drop alerts, callouts, active filters, and interactive highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#1C2833` - Deep Indigo Cobalt):** A deep, tailored tone utilized for high-priority utility elements, badges, and alternative dark blocks.
- **Deep Olive Black (`#2E3320`):** An earthy, dark charcoal-olive providing depth in subtle interactive states, button borders, and hover backgrounds.
- **Typographic Neutral (`#121214`):** Deep Obsidian Charcoal. Used for primary headlines, structural linework, and high-impact microcopy.
- **Structural Gray (`#E5E2DC`):** Muted warm hairline border color, framing grids and cards without visually cluttering the viewport.
- **Muted Subtext (`#706E6B`):** Mid-tone neutral for secondary specs, descriptions, breadcrumbs, and SKU tags.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs architectural display energy with Swiss-grade utilitarian clarity:

- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** Selected for its structural weight, sculptural curves, and raw edge. Syne is utilized for oversized drop banners, campaign manifestos, and lookbook section markers. Headlines always use tight negative tracking to maintain an imposing, premium fashion lockup.
- **Body, UI & Numbers (`Hanken Grotesk`):** A precise, contemporary grotesque engineered for seamless legibility across product cards, detailed specs, size selectors, and checkout flows.
- **Pricing Expression (₹ INR):** The Indian Rupee symbol is displayed using `Hanken Grotesk` medium or semi-bold weights to harmonize with numeric characters, avoiding standard glyph mismatches.
- **Microcopy & Metadata:** All collection tags, edition numbers, technical care instructions, and inventory status chips are set in `label-caps` (all-caps with wide tracking: `0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a responsive 1440px desktop grid, rooted in an 8pt architectural rhythm:

- **Desktop (1440px max viewport):** 12-column grid, `margin-desktop` (64px / 4rem), `gutter-desktop` (32px / 2rem). Outer boundaries align strictly with technical hairline borders.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column grid, `margin-tablet` (40px / 2.5rem), `gutter` (24px / 1.5rem).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin` (20px / 1.25rem), `gutter` (16px / 1rem). Product grids convert into an asymmetrical 2-column pattern or horizontal kinetic carousels.

Vertical spacing adheres to editorial pacing: generous pauses between thematic drops (`space-3xl`), structured separation between filters and catalogue items (`space-xl`), and dense, clinical spacing within operational controls (`space-xs` to `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy, diffused dropshadows. Depth is established through **architectural layering and structural borders**:

- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is defined by 1px borders in `#E5E2DC` surrounding white surfaces over the `#FBF9F5` base. This yields a crisp, tactile presence without digital fuzziness.
- **Interactive Layering:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` base background.
  - **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` solid fill bounded by 1px `#E5E2DC`.
  - **Level 2 (Hover & Floating State):** Cards translate `-4px` vertically with a precise, low-spread ambient shadow (`0 12px 24px -6px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.06)`).
  - **Level 3 (Drawers, Modals & Cart Flyouts):** Solid `#FFFFFF` or `#121214` surfaces with a 1px border. Underlaid with a high-contrast `#121214` scrim at 40% opacity (no blur), evoking physical stencil framing.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Sharp (`0`)** aesthetic philosophy:

- All cards, action buttons, filter chips, notifications, and input fields feature 0px border-radius (`rounded-none`).
- Sharp corners echo technical garment tags, architectural monoliths, and brutalist streetwear collateral.
- Where softer tactile elements are required (e.g., color swatch selectors or quantity steppers), geometry relies on pure circles (`50%` / circular tokens) to provide crisp geometric contrast against razor-sharp rectangular perimeters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Bag / Buy Now):** Solid `#121214` background, `#FBF9F5` text, 0px border radius, 52px height. Hover: Shifts to `#8FA842` background with `#121214` text; instant zero-lag transition.
- **Secondary (Explore Drop / View Lookbook):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#121214` border, `#121214` text. Hover: Solid `#121214` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Text-only with uppercase `label-caps` tracking and an underline that expands from center on hover.

### Product Cards
- Bounded by `#FFFFFF` fill and a 1px `#E5E2DC` hairline border.
- Image container preserves a 4:5 editorial aspect ratio with a subtle warm-tinted gray backdrop (`#F5F3EE`) for transparency handling.
- Secondary hover action triggers a clean fade to the alternate lookbook/model shot.
- Price line features ₹ INR notation in bold `Hanken Grotesk`, paired with an edition tag (e.g., "LIMITED EDITION [100 PCS]") in `label-caps`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Architectural rectangles with 1px `#E5E2DC` borders.
- Active state: `#121214` background with crisp white text.
- Accent state (Hot Drop / Trending): `#8FA842` background with `#121214` bold text.

### Form Inputs
- 1px bottom border only or full-framed 1px box in `#E5E2DC` over a `#FFFFFF` fill.
- Focus state: Border transitions to 1px `#121214` (no outer glow or halo).
- Placeholder text in `#706E6B`, shifting upward to `label-caps` when active.

### Checkboxes & Size Selectors
- **Size Selector:** Grid of square boxes (44px x 44px) with 1px border. Active selection fills `#121214` with white type. Out-of-stock sizes display a subtle 45-degree diagonal strike-through.
- **Checkboxes:** 18px x 18px square with a 1px border. Checked state displays a solid `#121214` fill with an architectural checkmark or solid square insert.

### Streetwear-Specific Modules
- **Drop Ticker:** Persistent marquee ticker at viewport edges displaying collection alerts, pop-up dates, and dispatch times in `label-caps`.
- **Quick-Cart Drawer:** Slide-in right panel with full-height hairline divider, zero border radius, and structured cart items separated by dashed hairline rules.
